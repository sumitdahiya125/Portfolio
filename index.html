About

I'm a backend engineer. I build the services that handle requests, design the data shapes they speak in, and own them end-to-end through production. Most of my work lives in Java, Rust, and Python.

At Juspay, I work on Hyperswitch — an open-source payments orchestrator handling real merchant traffic. I've shipped CUG (closed-user-group) auth flows serving thousands of daily transactions, migrated the service onto a new internal platform, and drove our PCI DSS recertification. I care about clear APIs, instrumented systems, and writing the smallest change that solves the problem.
